## Install

1. **Get Tampermonkey** for your browser: [Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tampermonkey/dhdgffkkebhmkfjojejmpbldmpobfkfo) · [Edge](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/tampermonkey/iikmkjmpaadaobahmlepeloendndfphd) · [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/tampermonkey/) · [Opera GX & others](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) (or use [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/))
2. **[Click here to install Blossom V4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Virojet/Shell-Shockers-Blossom-V4-Theme/main/Shell-Shockers-Blossom-V4.user.js)**, then press **Install** on the page that opens
3. **Reload [shellshock.io](https://shellshock.io)**

> [!TIP]
> **Theme not showing?** Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera need one extra switch: open your browser's **Extensions** page → **Tampermonkey** → **Details** → turn on **Allow User Scripts**. Then reload the game. [Step by step for each browser](https://github.com/Virojet/Shell-Shockers-Better-Hud-Mod#allow-user-scripts)

## What it changes

- **Home screen and menus** get the cherry blossom art behind everything, black and pink tabs, and dark text fields.
- **Popups** use the blossom art too: settings, game options, help, sign-in, private games, the tutorial and more.
- **Inventory and shop** tiles show the art, while premium, limited and VIP items keep their own colours so you can still tell them apart. The VIP club is themed too.
- **The loading screen** shows the blossom art with a pink loading bar.
- **In game** you get a pink crosshair, health bar, hard-boiled ring, streak rewards and chat.
- **Kill messages** read "YOU BLOSSOMED" and "YOU WERE BLOSSOMED BY".
- **Team colours stay the same**, so you can still tell teams apart.

## New since V3

- Works with the game's current menus and HUD. V3's rules for the old kill box, streak counter and shield no longer matched anything.
- Readable everywhere: no more white text on white or light pink in fields, dropdowns and buttons.
- Every menu checked, including ones V3 never reached: the VIP club, Twitch drops, Chick'n Winner, the tutorial, server ping bars and pinned chat.
- Loads at document start, so there's no flash of the normal look, and it no longer renames your browser tab.

## Good to know

- It's cosmetic only: it adds one stylesheet and runs no other code than the anonymous install count described under [Privacy](#privacy). It **updates by itself** through Tampermonkey.
- The background art and kill-streak picture load from [Foggier01/Blossom-V3-Fix-by-Foggier](https://github.com/Foggier01/Blossom-V3-Fix-by-Foggier), the same images Blossom V3 used. Nothing about you is sent anywhere.
- Works with [Better HUD](https://github.com/Virojet/Shell-Shockers-Better-Hud-Mod) (your crosshair settings still win) and shows up in the Mod Menu under Theme. Use one theme at a time, or the colours mix.
- To turn it off, switch it off in Tampermonkey and reload the game.

## Privacy

- **Install count:** the first time it runs, one anonymous hit goes to a public counter ([Abacus](https://abacus.jasoncameron.dev)) so the installs badge above stays current. No settings, names or IDs. It's sent once per browser; the `ssBlossomV4-install-counted` key in site storage remembers it.
